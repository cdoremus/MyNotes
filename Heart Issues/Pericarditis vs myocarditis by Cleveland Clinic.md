# Diagnosis and Management of Pericarditis

[Allan Klein, MD](https://providers.clevelandclinic.org/provider/allan-klein/4268446), and [Deborah Kwon, MD](https://providers.clevelandclinic.org/provider/deborah-kwon/4268282), discuss contemporary guidance on pericarditis with emphasis on evolving diagnostic criteria, inflammatory syndromes and the role of multimodality imaging. The conversation explores risk stratification, medical treatment options and surgical decision-making in complex and recurrent disease.
